# What I worked on
This week I spent a lot of time building out the front-end pages of our web-application. Specifically, I created the entire real-estate agent persona where you can view and create listings, as well as view the EU market as a whole in the market dashboard. I am very proud of the house price index visualization, because I feel like it shows the data very accurately and clearly for each country, so a real estate agent would be able to predict the approximate growth or decline in the next few years. I also added more to the student persona, with the reviews page now having an option of adding, editing, or deleting reviews. To do this, I had to add a user_id to the reviews table, since I didn’t want any user to be able to edit any review. Though, the users are still publicly anonymous. I also did some work with the design of the homepage and sidebar. Throughout the week, I had to add or alter a few new routes, like the get_cities route had to be modified to be sorted by country for the listings filters. I generated more data for listings and reviews, making sure that each listing has at least three reviews on it to add variety to the averages. Each country has at least one listing. The listings pages are sorted automatically by the highest rating now, as well. 

# What I saw
This week, I enjoyed our trip to the chocolate shop where we got to design our own chocolate and add our own toppings. I found it very interesting to learn about the process of harvesting the chocolate, and even tried some of the cacao nibs, which I did not like. On Sunday, I visited Antwerpen, which was a fairly boring city. Though, they did have a Dunkin Donuts, which was an exciting discovery. I liked learning about the miners in Genk and getting to see different parts of the tunnels, as well as the view from the top of the coal tower. The climb up was less than pleasant, but it was nice to be able to see the city from so high, especially seeing the windmills after learning about them earlier that day.
