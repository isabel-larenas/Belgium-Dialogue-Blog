# My first blog post

My experience so far on this dialogue has been great! I have learned a lot about the government in the European Union and how it is structured. Before coming here, I did not know nearly as much about the government as I do now. 

I loved when we went on a walking tour of Leuven and got to learn about the history of the town. I thought it was funny how there was a tree that was said to grow three times larger than expected due to the women crying in the courtyard when they left their boyfriends for school. I also love all the food I have tried here so far, including Roots, Nona, Boccone, Pain Louvain, and the Belgian waffles! It has all been so tasty.

For the Phase 1 Deliverable, I wrote the project description and gathered information about each dataset. This will be important for the future for when we need to clean the datasets and produce our final product.
