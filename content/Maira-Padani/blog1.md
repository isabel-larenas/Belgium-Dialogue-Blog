# My first blog post

Our most recent visit to the EEAS particularly stood out to me throughout the course of this dialogue. Learning about FIMI (Foreign Information Manipulation and Interference) and how the EEAS uses data to mitigate disinformation campaigns made me think differently about the information we are constantly exposed to online as well as the constant data we work with in class. For Phase 1, I developed the government agency persona, Emma Maria Berg, a project manager at the European Social Fund Plus. I focused on how someone like her would use housing and poverty data to make funding allocation decisions. I also helped research relevant datasets within Eurostat, specifically "Housing cost overburden rate by age, sex and poverty status" and "At-risk-of-poverty rate after deducting housing costs by degree of urbanisation."
