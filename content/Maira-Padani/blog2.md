# What I worked on
For phase II of the project, I created the Student ER model, and Geo focused on the other two. We both collaborated a lot throughout the entirety of the creation process for the localized ER diagrams, making sure all three were consistent, comparable, and could be collaborated into the global ER diagram. After we finished the localized ER diagrams, we combined them into the global ER diagram. We were first hand drawing them but moved to lucid chart to depict more concrete diagrams. We intially started out with many attributes but narrowed them down to fewer as we continued the process. Specifically, we combined the separate entities of different stats and created two entities of social indicator type and social indicator stats to condense our information. On the technical side, I created the main page for phase 2 updates, wrote about updated user personas, and wrote summaries depciting relationships between entities, explaining cardinality and participation. I also updated the phase 2 page with the global ER diagram, global DDL, SQL code, and wireframes. In terms of the wireframes, I created the original real estate and government agency wireframes that were modified by Laasya and finetuned.
