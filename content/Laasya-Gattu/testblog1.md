# My first blog post

So far on the dialogue, I learned a lot about the European Union's governmental structure and how the different sectors work together with elements of democracy. I particularly enjoyed the visit to the European Commission and the talk by Giulia Geneletti on the Digital Security Act, because I thought it was very relevant to the risks of today's digital landscape. The conversation on AI image generation and minor protections was very interesting and I like how it led into a larger topic on how the EU prioritizes proper data usage and privacy regulations. 

For the Phase 1 deliverable, I was responsible for the user persona of Noah Bernard, who represents a student archetype user of our app. I created the 4 user stories for his persona which included filtering, sorting, and comparing housing options for his purposes as a student renter. I also contributed to searching for relevant Eurostat datasets, specifically ![Share of people living in under-occupied dwellings by household type and income quintile](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/ilc_lvho50b__custom_21476751/default/table) and ![Noise from neighbours or from the street by degree of urbanisation](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/ilc_mddw04__custom_21476822/default/table).
