## Dialogue Week 2

For the DS portion of the project, Isabel and I collaborated on the data pipeline for creating the POC ML model. We did most of the work together, such as running the API calls for each of the Eurostat datasets, cleaning the datasets, and doing basic EDA. Individually, after merging the 4 datasets we were interested in using for the k-NN model, I worked more on the feature engineering of the merged dataset, such as scaling the data first to make sure the data would make sense across datasets, then writing the functions to calculate specific "rates" for crime, noise, pollution; and a "weight" for housing price index. This helped when I was putting together the knn model, which took a lot of trial and error, as explained in the team phase 2 blog post. I also made the plots for finding the best k to use in the model, by trying both cosine similarity and l2 norm. For the wireframes, I created the student persona wireframes, and then also helped clean up the government agency manager wireframes as well. 

Outside of class, I enjoyed visiting the atomium, Brugge, and Ghent, both places were really nice and I loved seeing the historic architecture and visiting the local markets. I'm looking forward to visiting Strasbourg and Luxembourg this weekend!
